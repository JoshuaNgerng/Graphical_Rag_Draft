We chunk the source text, generate embeddings for the chunks, 
extract entities and relationships, and store the structured knowledge in Neo4j. 
The chunks remain the source evidence and are linked to the entities/relationships. 
At query time, we can use vector similarity and/or entity/relationship traversal to find the relevant graph paths and source chunks, 
then give that combined context to the LLM.
