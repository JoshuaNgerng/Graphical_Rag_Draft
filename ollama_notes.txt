If you want to estimate it yourself, think:

RAM≈W+KV+O

where:

W = model weights
KV = KV cache
O = runtime overhead
Weights

Approximately:

W=P×Q/8

where:

P = number of parameters
Q = bits per parameter

So:

7B Q4:

7×10
9
×4/8≈3.5GB

Actual ≈ 4–5 GB.

14B Q4:

14×10
9
×4/8≈7GB

Actual ≈ 8–10 GB.

32B Q4:

32×10
9
×4/8≈16GB

Actual ≈ 20+ GB.

That's why your 16 GB Mac can't realistically run a 32B Q4 model.
